# Pinged product contract

Recovered from the owner's October 13–15 discussions. This is the acceptance contract, not a list of completed features.

- Brand: Pinged. Minimal Apple-inspired unified dashboard, Ocean Teal; headline “What would you like to stay updated on?” and inspiration heading “Discover what Pinged can do”.
- Conversational creation and editing, automatic relevant-source suggestions, review before activation. Help the user identify their three most useful specific Pings with minimal onboarding.
- Free: three ACTIVE Pings, up to three sources per Ping. Pausing frees a slot; resuming must recheck the limit. Unlimited saved ideas do not consume slots. Disclose full capacity before recommending activation.
- Free includes silent in-app folder delivery or push notifications. Notification preferences must be honored. Never simulate push/email success.
- Free and Pro only; no advertising or paid recommendations. Pro is optional and contextual, with broader coverage, related-interest discovery and longer searchable history. No approved price recorded; do not invent one. Historical planner intervals and numerical Pro limits are provisional implementation defaults, not owner-approved commitments.
- Interest learning is optional, reviewable and resettable; useful/not-useful feedback must never silently change explicit monitoring criteria. New suggested Pings require approval.
- Accepted experience backlog: Important / Every Discovery / Daily Summary, separate inbox/email controls, automatic recovery, smart collections, intelligent suggestions, integrated timeline, save/compare, verified details, conversational editing, universal command bar and rich notification cards.
- Monitor multiple relevant websites for news, listings and opportunities. Every source must expose real capability and failures. A suggested source is not a connected integration. Extracted grade/price/year are not verified listing facts.
- Optimize long-term profit behind the scenes while keeping free useful, quality high, experience generous and trust intact.

## Release gates

An MVP is not complete until creation → persistence → scheduled source check → exact matching → deduplication → inbox → chosen notification works in the running deployment. Account isolation, pause/resume, free limits, failures/retries, restart persistence, responsive/accessibility QA and supported-source capability checks must pass. Push/email, billing and production accounts require real implementations and configuration. Never report isolated unit tests as a complete running-app test.
